Present Simple: Daily Routines

Routine

Prepositions: Where Is It?

Place

Where is the cat?

4

Present Simple vs Present Continuous

Compare

I like chocolate. (Present Simple - general truth)

I am eating chocolate now. (Present Continuous - happening now)

My brother plays football every Saturday. (Present Simple - habit)

My brother is playing football right now. (Present Continuous - action now)

Which sentence is for something happening now?
